# PRODUCTS.md — the product map

Atlearn in one line: **a free library of learning roadmaps — step-by-step
paths for real-world skills, written and tended by a community, every map
kept by a named maintainer.**

This is the product manager's sheet. It says what each product does for
people, what it promises, and where it stands — in plain language. The
companion documents go deeper: GOVERNANCE.md holds the principles,
CATALOG.md the build-level inventory of every surface, JOURNEYS.md the
walkable proof that flows work. Read this file first; drill down from it.

**Status marks** — the app is complete but not yet launched, so "in the
product" means in the build today:
● in the product · ◐ built, wakes up when its first real data exists ·
○ planned — decided, dated, not yet built.

## 1 · The Library — reading

**For:** everyone, signed in or not.
**Promise:** the whole library is free to read, forever — no account, no
tracking, nothing asked of the visitor.

- ● Home shelf — every map as a spare card; your active paths pinned on
  top with the next real-world action, once you have any.
- ● The map — a visual path per skill: core topics down the spine,
  subtopics branching off. It reads at natural size on any screen —
  scrolling is the only gesture, nothing to zoom or learn. Every topic
  opens a card with a short lesson, 1–2 trusted free links, and a
  concrete do-this-week action.
- ● Search — find a topic by name; when names run out, search reaches
  into summaries and actions.
- ● The Atlas — browse all maps by honest, explainable criteria
  (practice map, endorsed, seeking a maintainer…), never by popularity.
- ● Category pages — each skill lists its official map first, then every
  community version, Wikipedia-style. While a skill has no community
  versions the page steps aside: the shelf opens the map directly, and
  "⑂ all versions" on the map is the way in.
- ● "Changed since your last visit" — returning walkers see what moved,
  in dates and plain words.
- ● Every page has a shareable address; links land exactly where they say.
- ● Light and dark — the library reads on paper by default, with a
  one-tap dark mode; the choice sticks to the device and, signed in, to
  the account.

**Deliberate limits** — no feed, no rankings, no ads, no version numbers
or stats on reading pages. Sensitive maps (health, finance, first aid)
carry scope disclaimers and stay educational only.

## 2 · Progress & Notes

**For:** signed-in walkers.
**Promise:** your progress belongs to you — saved to your account the
moment you act, the same on every device, exportable and deletable.

- ● Topic status — not started / in progress / done, one tap.
- ● Checkable actions — ticking your first step moves the topic to "in
  progress" automatically.
- ● Private notes on any topic.
- ● Progress bars on the shelf; "Continue your paths" on the home page.
- ● Saves go to the account's server record; the app shows true save
  state (saving / saved / couldn't save — retrying) and never pretends.
- ● Without a connected account (demo copies), nothing saves — and the
  app says so plainly instead of faking it.

**Deliberate limits** — visitors can tick actions and set status to feel
the loop, but the marks last only the visit (nothing is stored for the
signed-out; one quiet nudge says so, and signing in brings the visit's
marks along). Notes need an account; walker behavior is never counted,
compared, or ranked.

*Detail: CATALOG §4 · Proof: J7, J10.*

## 3 · The Journal

**For:** signed-in walkers.
**Promise:** a private place to record what happened when you actually
did the thing. Yours alone, never public.

- ● Dated entries, filterable by map; deep-linkable composer.
- ● Optional streak display (off by default).
- ● Copy everything as text; deleting an entry erases it everywhere.

**Deliberate limits** — deliberately tucked away: reachable only from the
account page. No journal buttons or prompts on maps.

*Detail: CATALOG §8 · Proof: J8.*

## 4 · Contributing — the wiki door

**For:** any signed-in user.
**Promise:** anyone can propose an improvement to the shared map, in the
same editor the maintainer uses — and nothing publishes without a named
human deciding.

- ● Edit any topic — for non-owners, Save files a proposal to the map's
  maintainer, with an exact before/after view.
- ● Share a field-tested tip — free-text community wisdom; approved tips
  display beside the curated content, clearly labeled. Fixes, better
  links, and missing subtopics go through the same editor as everything
  else (one door per intent — the edit door above).
- ● Flag a resource — one tap says a link is dead, stale, or unhelpful.
- ● Report content — safety reports jump a red queue with a 48-hour norm.
- ● Your contributions page — status of everything you've filed, withdraw
  while pending, and every rejection carries a written reason.
- ● Merged contributions carry your name into the public record, forever.

**Deliberate limits** — affiliation must be disclosed; paywalled or
tracking-tagged links are refused; community tips never mix into the
curated lesson.

*Detail: CATALOG §4, 15 · Proof: J11–J14.*

## 5 · Personal versions — branches

**For:** any signed-in user.
**Promise:** if you'd teach it differently, make your own version —
public, walkable by anyone, without a fight over the original.

- ● One branch per map per person; edit it freely with the full editor.
- ● Born unlisted: a new branch is yours alone, shareable by link, and
  reaches the map's public version list only when you publish it — the
  public space cannot be filled by accident.
- ● An intro paragraph in your own words, rendered to every visitor like
  the official map's lead.
- ● A one-line subtitle in your own words — "the beginner version" — on
  your branch's page and its row on the category page, so walkers know
  what makes it different before opening it.
- ● Topics you haven't touched keep tracking the official map; any topic
  can be reset to standard.
- ● Share by link; your walking progress carries across versions.
- ● Listed on the map's category page and counted in the Atlas.
- ○ "Offer this to the trunk" — turn your branch's changes into proposals
  for the official map, in one step (decided 2026-08-09).

**Deliberate limits** — branches never alter the official map; the
feature stays quiet by design — suggesting improvements is the preferred
path.

*Detail: CATALOG §5 · Proof: J15, J16.*

## 6 · Collections

**For:** any signed-in user.
**Promise:** curate a shelf of maps — the set you'd hand a friend.

- ● Create, edit, delete your own shelves; browse and search everyone's.
- ● Admins can feature a great shelf or hide a bad one — nothing else.

**Deliberate limits** — shelves point at maps; they can never change one.

*Detail: CATALOG §7.*

## 7 · The Review desk

**For:** maintainers, their appointed helpers (stewards), admins.
**Promise:** an accountable editor's desk — a fast yes or no, in public.

- ● One queue per role, scoped to your maps: proposed edits with plain
  before/after diffs, suggestions, resource flags, reports.
- ● Merge publishes instantly; reject requires a written reason the
  author sees.
- ● Structural changes (add/remove/reorder topics) wait seven days with
  an open comment thread; rejection never waits.
- ● Stewards handle small fixes only; bigger calls stay with the
  maintainer.
- ● Safety reports sort first, marked red, 48-hour norm.
- ● Automation (like the link steward) files drafts marked 🤖 — only a
  human can merge, ever.

**Deliberate limits** — no one moderates outside their scope; empty
powers are hidden, not greyed out; there is no anonymous decision.

*Detail: CATALOG §10 · Proof: J17–J19, J25.*

## 8 · Governance

**For:** admins, maintainers, the community around a map.
**Promise:** every grant of authority is a named, public, one-line record
— and every map has exactly one accountable voice.

- ● Roles ladder — walkers → contributors → stewards → maintainers →
  admins → superadmin; each level binds the one below, in the open.
- ● Orphaned maps stay live and readable, banner up, taking adoption
  applications; a designated successor holds first refusal.
- ● Maintainers name successors; admins confirm handovers.
- ○ Co-maintainership — a team or institution holds a map jointly
  (decided 2026-08-09).
- ○ The stewardship review — when a community outgrows an active
  maintainer: contributors nominate by signed endorsement, admins
  confirm; moves the binding, never the content; at most one review per
  map per year (decided 2026-08-09).

**Deliberate limits** — popularity is never authority; elections don't
run standing; root access is never grantable from the app.

*Detail: CATALOG §2, 9 · Proof: J20, J21, J27 · The constitution: GOVERNANCE.md.*

## 9 · Guilds

**For:** communities around a subject area. ◐ Built; wakes when the
first guild is founded.
**Promise:** community without authority — a hall, not a committee.

- ◐ A hall per guild: blurb, guides, member maps, open talk thread.
- ◐ Endorsements — the system's only comparative judgment: a guide signs
  a dated, reasoned mark of quality on a member map; withdrawable.
- ○ Endorsing community branches, not just official maps (decided
  2026-08-09).

**Deliberate limits** — guides tend conversation and sign endorsements;
they cannot edit, merge, or bind anyone.

*Detail: CATALOG §11 · Proof: J26.*

## 10 · Account & Privacy

**For:** signed-in walkers.
**Promise:** we hold as little as possible, and you can leave with
everything, without asking anyone.

- ● Sign in with Google (no password created or seen) or with email +
  password (created on first use; reset by email link).
- ● Download my data — everything, one file, one click.
- ● Delete my account — self-service, removes progress, notes, journal,
  branches, shelves, and pending contributions. Merged contributions
  remain in the public record, credited, as published speech.
- ● The privacy page is the complete inventory of what exists about you.

**Deliberate limits** — no analytics, no ad tech, no selling data, no
tracking of readers, nothing stored for the signed-out.

*Detail: CATALOG §9, 14 · Proof: J9.*

## 11 · Content operations — the team's product

**For:** authors, maintainers, the project itself.
**Promise:** the library stays healthy with automation on staff and
humans in charge.

- ● In-app authoring on the team's machine writes real content files,
  validated as it saves.
- ● Every merged change lands in the public repository with the
  contributor's and merger's names attached.
- ◐ The link steward — when a vetted link dies, automation drafts the
  swap from a pre-approved successor list; a human merges. Wakes when
  links carry successor lists.
- ● Nightly link checking across every resource in the library.
- ● Vital signs — the numbers the project holds itself to, computed from
  the repository only; reader behavior counted nowhere.

**Deliberate limits** — automation never edits directly and never
merges; all counting is of content, not people.
